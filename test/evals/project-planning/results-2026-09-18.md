# Planning guidance evaluation — September 18, 2026

## Method and historical basis

An independent evaluator received only the generated instructions, routed references, and synthetic `inputs.json`. Cases A–E used the project-planning skill; F used the frontend-planner prompt. No rubric, source diff, intended fix, or prior answer was supplied. Outputs are drafts, not native BCO tasks. No live workflow, database, browser, hosting service, or project was changed. Raw outputs are retained locally under `.tmp-ai-planning/evaluation/`.

The scenarios reflect the LENTO initial-release plan and its cycle-corrected, preview-revised, requirements-filled, and asset-production revisions. Those records showed an initially unbound capability/resource draft, a parent blocked by its descendant, a missing form-consumer edge, planner-only media assumptions, and a private brief path. Later release recovery exposed incompatible read-only, harness-repair, and candidate-delivery scope. The original brief requested a manual screen-reader smoke; later user instructions removed human walkthroughs. Publication was deferred separately. These are evidence for general reasoning guidance, not permanent technology or task-name rules.

The initial generated text is identified by [initial source hashes](initial-source-hashes.json). Hashes normalize CRLF to LF. The evaluator performed its own limited local checks; the primary agent also loaded BCO's existing pure `validateNativeProjectPlan` function offline to check schema, graph, and role contracts. Its capability-set argument was synthetic (all declarations) to isolate these checks; this establishes neither real availability nor admission. It is not the canonical preview with semantic audit and runtime evidence.
